# Style Guide

## Visual Direction

Use a MathWorks-like technical style: light background, subtle grid, strong blue/orange/yellow accents, crisp black text, and generous whitespace. Prefer purposeful diagrams and large visual moments over generic cards.

## Palette

- Ink: `#1f1f1f`
- Muted: `#555f66`
- Paper: `#f5f7fa`
- MATLAB-like blue: `#0076a8`
- Deep blue: `#004b87`
- Orange: `#d9531e`
- Yellow: `#edb120`

## Layout

- Use one dominant message per slide.
- Prefer two-column layouts for explanation plus visual.
- Use full-width visual slides for opening hooks.
- Keep captions large enough to read from a meeting room screen.
- Avoid relying on tiny text inside screenshots or borrowed images; recreate labels in HTML.

## Motion

Use spring/jelly animation for cards and visual groups. Motion should feel physical: move, overshoot, settle. Avoid constant motion after elements settle.

## Avoid

- Purple-on-white generic AI styling.
- Dense bullet lists.
- Small screenshots without readable labels.
- Decorative icons that do not support the message.
